    FAQ

    Questions Facility Managers Ask

    How much does warehouse security cost?

    Pricing depends on whether you need on-site guards or mobile patrols, armed or unarmed officers, the hours (nights, weekends, 24/7) and the size of your site. Request a free quote and we will give you an exact price – no obligation.

    Should we choose an on-site guard or mobile patrols?

    On-site guards are best when you need constant access control, dock monitoring or a visible presence during shifts. Mobile patrols visit your site at varied times and are a cost-effective option for after-hours coverage. Many sites combine both – your consultant will recommend the right mix.

    Do we need armed or unarmed officers?

    Most warehouses and industrial sites are well served by unarmed officers who control access, monitor shipping and receiving and patrol the site. Armed officers are recommended for high-value inventory or after specific threats. We will recommend the right option during your free consultation.

    Are your officers licensed and insured?

    Yes. Top Notch Security Solutions is licensed by the California Bureau of Security and Investigative Services (PPO #122479). Our officers hold valid BSIS guard cards, and we are fully bonded and insured.

    How quickly can a guard start?

    For urgent situations we can often have officers on site within hours. Call our 24/7 line at (925) 440-6909 if you need someone right away.

    What happens after I submit the form?

    Your request goes straight to our team. A licensed security consultant calls you within 2 hours during business hours to understand your site and risks, then sends you a custom security plan and quote. The consultation is free and there is no obligation.

    FAQ

    Fire Watch Questions

    What is a fire watch?

    A fire watch is a trained officer on site whose job is to look for fire hazards, patrol the property, respond immediately to any sign of fire and keep a detailed log. It is typically used while fire alarm or sprinkler systems are out of service, during hot work, or on construction and renovation projects.

    When is a fire watch required?

    Fire marshals and insurers commonly require a fire watch when a building’s fire alarm or sprinkler system is impaired, during hot work such as welding or cutting, and on some construction sites. If you have been told you need one, call us – we will help you meet the requirement.

    How quickly can fire watch guards start?

    Fire watch is often needed right away. Call our 24/7 line at (925) 440-6909 for urgent requests, or request a quote and tell us your start time – we will do our best to staff it as fast as possible.

    What do your fire watch guards do on site?

    They patrol the property on a set schedule, check for fire hazards, respond immediately to alarms or signs of fire, alert occupants and emergency services when needed, and keep detailed fire watch logs you can show the fire marshal or insurer.

    How much does fire watch cost?

    Pricing depends on how many guards you need, the hours and the number of days. Request a free quote and we will give you an exact price – no obligation.

    Are your guards licensed and insured?

    Yes. Top Notch Security Solutions is licensed by the California Bureau of Security and Investigative Services (PPO #122479). Our guards are trained in fire safety and emergency procedures, and we are fully bonded and insured.
